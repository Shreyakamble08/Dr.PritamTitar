About the Chakan Clinic

Dr. Pritam Titar Heart Clinic in Chakan offers comprehensive, evidence-based cardiac care with a strong focus on accurate diagnosis, preventive cardiology, and ethical medical advice. The clinic is led by Dr. Pritam Titar, an experienced interventional cardiologist, ensuring every patient receives expert evaluation and clear, honest guidance.

Services Available

  • ✔ Cardiac Consultation
  • ✔ Echocardiography (2D Echo)
  • ✔ Electrocardiography (ECG)
  • ✔ Treadmill Test (TMT)
  • ✔ Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM)
  • ✔ Holter Monitoring (24–48 hour ECG)
  • ✔ Diet and lifestyle advice for heart health
  • ✔ Genuine second opinions for cardiac conditions and procedures

Why Choose This Clinic

  • ✔ Specialist-led care
  • ✔ Accurate testing and interpretation
